\section*{Abstract}
This bachelor's thesis focuses on the conceptualization, hardware design, and firmware implementation of an 
autonomous LoRaWAN sensor node prototype for deployment in hazardous areas. The prototype is designed to transmit 
data measured by its gas sensors to a public LoRaWAN network. Transmission ranges are empirically determined 
and compared against theoretical limits. In the course of the field tests conducted within this work, 
transmission distances of up to \qty{6}{km} were successfully achieved in the urban environment of Berlin.
