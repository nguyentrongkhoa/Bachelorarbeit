PCB-Prototype design:
    - Think about matching circuits
    - Follow ST reference design
    - For coverage testing: use U.FL (IPEX) connectors for easy antenna swapping
    - Use TCXO since LoRa is sensitive to freq Shift
    - GPS/GNSS: use u-blox MAX-10S or Quectel L76-LB due to low power (with MOSFET control)
    - MAX10S for example requires VDD to be stable before Uart goes high 
    - Use energy harvesting IC rather than LDO or normal buck conv
    - SPV1050 from ST: for solar charging with MPPT (max power point tracking)
    - BQ25570 from TI: for solar charging with MPPT, but not available in Germany
    - For battery: use 18650 Li-ion or LiFePO4 for better safety, but they are bigger than coin cells
    - external watchdog to prevent LoRa stack form hanging
    - SPI flash chip to log data locally in case of network failure
    - Keep GPS traces away from 868MHz RF traces
    - Add a small LDO (e.g. LP5907, 1 µA quiescent) between main rail and RF VDD. 
      This reduces ripple on the RF supply which measurably improves receiver sensitivity
    - Use a battery fuel gauge IC (e.g. MAX17048) to monitor battery state of charge without needing a shunt resistor,
      SoC can also be included in payload for long deployments
    - Rubber duck (helical) antenna very popular for gateways
